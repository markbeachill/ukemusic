Brahms' Lullaby

Rights: The tune is Brahms' "Wiegenlied" (Lullaby), Op. 49 No. 4, published in 1868. The English words ("Lullaby, and good night") are a 19th-century translation sung in many old songbooks; Brahms died in 1897. All public domain. The German words are from Des Knaben Wunderhorn (1808) and a verse by Georg Scherer (1849), also public domain, if you'd like to add them.
Arrangement: in C, in 3/4. The first half stays on C and then G7, the second half goes to F (Lay thee down). Set to be picked (Pick → Up and back): g C E A E C fits a bar of 3/4 exactly.
