Drunken Sailor

Rights: Traditional sea shanty. Words from Richard Runciman Terry, The Shanty Book, Part I (1921), no. 14 (verses 1 to 3); Terry died in 1938. Public domain in the UK. His piano accompaniment is not used.
Chords: a new beginner arrangement, from Beginner ChordPro (02 Drunken Sailor.cho); see its README.md for the sources.
Beginner arrangement - two chords in a minor key.
Tip: Keep a steady pulse through Am and G. Start slowly; speed is optional.
