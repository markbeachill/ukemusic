Ode to Joy

Rights: The tune is the "Ode to Joy" theme from the last movement of Beethoven's Ninth Symphony (1824). The English words are the first two verses of Henry van Dyke's "Hymn of Joy" (1907), written for this tune; van Dyke died in 1933. All public domain. Only the famous theme is here, as a song to strum or pick, not the symphony.
Arrangement: in C, one chord a bar, with the third phrase going to G7 (the bridge). Set to be picked (Pick → Up, twice a bar): a good first song for fingerpicking.
