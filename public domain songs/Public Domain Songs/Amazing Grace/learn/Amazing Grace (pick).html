The pattern

One string a beatg C E+A

Thumb, index, middle, ring: one string a beat, from the top string down. The way in: just hold the chord and keep time.

Fingerp·i·m+a·
Stringg·C·E+A·

Fingers: p thumb · i index · m middle · a ring. + means picked together; · is a rest. Hold the whole chord shape and pick from it: every fret number in the tab is already under a finger.

How to learn it

  1. Hold one chord and play the pattern over and over, slowly, until your picking hand does it on its own.
  2. Change chords on beat 1: the new shape has to be down by the time your thumb picks.
  3. Then the song, slowly (Pick → Play, with the practice speed turned down), up to speed over a few days.
