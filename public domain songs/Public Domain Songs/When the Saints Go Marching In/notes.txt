When the Saints Go Marching In

Rights: Traditional African American spiritual (not the different 1896 hymn). Public domain. Verses 1, 2 and 4 of the traditional words.
Chords: a new beginner arrangement, from Beginner ChordPro (05 When the Saints Go Marching In.cho); see its README.md for the sources.
Beginner arrangement - hearing the return home.
Tip: Sing the opening "Oh, when the" as a pickup before the first full bar.
Tip: Listen for G7 leading back to C. Play straight before trying a gentle swing.
